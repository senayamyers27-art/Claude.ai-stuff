Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

This page explains what information 11:Eleven Lounge ("11:Eleven," "we," "us") collects through this website, and how it's used. We keep this simple on purpose: we're a lounge, not a data company.

Information we collect

When you request a reservation through this site, the details you enter — name, phone number and/or email, party size, and requested date/time — are submitted directly to SevenRooms, the third-party reservation platform we use to manage bookings.

Separately, our host and floor staff use an internal reservation system to run the room night-to-night — seating guests, tracking the walk-in list, and noting table assignments. That system stores reservation details (name, party size, time, table, and any notes like a VIP or celebration tag) on our own servers, restricted to authenticated staff logins. It's how the floor gets run once you're through the door; it isn't shared outside the venue.

If you contact us directly (phone, email, or in person), we keep whatever information is reasonably needed to respond to you or honor a reservation.

Analytics

Like most websites, we may use a privacy-conscious analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall visit trends — pages viewed, general location by city/region, and device type. This data is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally. You can opt out at any time using your browser's cookie or tracking settings, or tools like Google's Analytics Opt-out extension.

How we use information

  • To confirm and manage your reservation
  • To respond to questions or requests you send us
  • To understand how visitors use this website, so we can improve it

We do not sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties beyond what's needed to fulfill a reservation (i.e., SevenRooms) or as required by law.

Third-party services

Our reservation system is operated by SevenRooms, which has its own privacy practices governing information submitted through the booking flow. We encourage you to review SevenRooms' own privacy policy for details on how they handle reservation data.

Data retention & security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, and rely on our service providers' security practices (including SevenRooms' and our hosting provider's) to protect it in transit and at rest.

In our internal reservation system, completed or cancelled reservations are automatically deleted after 1 year, resolved walk-in entries after 30 days, and aggregate nightly performance figures after 2 years. Access to that system is limited to authenticated staff accounts.

Your choices

You can decline to submit personal information to us at any time — note that doing so may mean we're unable to process a reservation request. To ask a question about this policy or request that we delete information you've sent us directly, contact us using the information below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as our practices evolve. The "Effective" date above reflects the most recent revision.
