Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 August 2026 · 11:Eleven Lounge, Uptown / Knox-Henderson, Dallas, Texas

This policy covers the information we collect when you reserve a table on our website, in the 11:Eleven app, or by phone — and what we do with it. We keep as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Your name and mobile number, so we know who to expect and can reach you about your table.
  • Reservation details — date, time, party size, the table you chose, and any note you send the host, including an occasion or dietary request.
  • Device token, only if you allow notifications in the app, so we can send alerts about your reservation.
  • Basic usage information — the pages or screens you open, so we can fix what is broken.
  • Details kept on your own device — if you switch on “keep my number for next time”, your name, mobile number and any dietary tags are saved in the app’s storage on your phone so you do not retype them. They stay on the device, we never receive that copy, and switching the setting off deletes it.

We do not collect payment card details through the website or the app. VIP minimum spend is settled in person at the venue.

Why we collect it

To hold your table, to contact you about it, and to set the room the way you asked. We also keep your past reservations so a returning guest can be offered their usual booth. Nothing is used for any other purpose.

Text messages and notifications

When you give us your number and confirm a reservation, you agree to receive text messages about that reservation — confirmations, reminders, and a message when a walk-in table is ready. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Reply STOP to any message to opt out of all texts, or HELP for help. In the app you can turn individual alert types off under “You”. Event announcements are off unless you switch them on. Opting out of reservation messages may mean we cannot tell you when your table is ready.

Who else sees it

We share what is necessary with service providers who act only on our instructions:

  • SevenRooms, which operates our reservation system.
  • Our text messaging provider, to deliver messages you have agreed to.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising or with data brokers. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Reservation records for two years, then they are deleted. Ask us sooner and we delete them sooner.

Your choices

Email privacy@11elevendallas.com to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you. We reply within 45 days. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, including the right to confirm, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, and opt out of certain processing of personal data — exercised through the same address.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and access is limited to staff who need it to seat you. No system is perfect; we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information.

Age

The 11:Eleven app is intended for guests 17 and over, and the venue is 21 and over after 9 PM. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we post the new version here with a new date. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be announced in the app.
