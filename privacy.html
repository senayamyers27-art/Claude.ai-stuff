Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

This page explains what information 11:Eleven Lounge ("11:Eleven," "we," "us") collects through this website, and how it's used. We keep this simple on purpose: we're a lounge, not a data company.

Information we collect

When you request a reservation through this site, the details you enter — name, phone number and/or email, party size, requested date/time, and any notes you add — are stored directly in our own reservation system, hosted on Cloudflare, so our staff can confirm your table and so you can look up or cancel your reservation later using the confirmation code you're given. We don't use a third-party booking platform.

If you contact us directly (phone, email, or in person), we keep whatever information is reasonably needed to respond to you or honor a reservation.

Analytics

Like most websites, we may use a privacy-conscious analytics tool (such as Google Analytics) to understand overall visit trends — pages viewed, general location by city/region, and device type. This data is aggregated and isn't used to identify you personally. You can opt out at any time using your browser's cookie or tracking settings, or tools like Google's Analytics Opt-out extension.

How we use information

  • To confirm and manage your reservation
  • To respond to questions or requests you send us
  • To understand how visitors use this website, so we can improve it

We do not sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties except as required by law.

Data retention & security

Completed or cancelled reservations are kept for up to 1 year, then automatically deleted; the same applies to related records like waitlist entries. Our reservation database is hosted on Cloudflare and encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Guest profile information (name, email/phone, visit history) built up from repeat reservations is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

You can decline to submit personal information to us at any time — note that doing so may mean we're unable to process a reservation request. To ask a question about this policy or request that we delete information you've sent us directly, contact us using the information below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time as our practices evolve. The "Effective" date above reflects the most recent revision.
