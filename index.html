The Concept

Dallas's calm, elegant lounge

11:Eleven pairs blush and warm neutral tones with brass, gold, and marble accents, deep velvet booth seating, and low ambient lighting — a room built for hours-long evenings, not a rushed night out. Curved booths, communal low tables, and a private VIP platform give the space a sense of occasion without ever feeling loud or crowded.
