CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 · Domain 3: Tools and documentation

Core project documents: project management plan, schedule, RACI, risk register, issue log, change log and assumption log

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed October 7, 2026 · Leer en español

Explain it simpler

Projects keep their information in a set of standard documents, and each one has its own job. The project management plan is the master rulebook: what the targets are and how the project will be run. The schedule shows what happens when. A RACI chart shows who does each task, who owns it, who is asked for advice and who is just kept informed. Then there are running logs that get updated all the time. The risk register lists things that might go wrong. The issue log lists problems happening now. The change log lists every request to change the plan. The assumption log lists things the team believes are true but has not checked yet. It is like a household: a budget plan, a calendar, a chore chart and a list of repairs, each kept in its own place.

Your first morning as project manager on the phone system replacement at Willow Creek Insurance. The sponsor wants to know why the go-live date in her calendar does not match the one the team quotes. A network engineer insists the firewall design is 'not his job'. Someone mentions that the vendor is late with handsets again, and a business analyst asks whether anyone ever confirmed that every office has the same cabling. Each of these questions has an answer, and each answer lives in a specific project document. Do you know which one to open for each, and which one to update next?

Project+ expects you to know which document holds which information, because many exam questions describe a need and ask where to look or what to update. Project documents fall into two broad groups. Plans describe how the project will be run and what the targets are. Logs and registers track what is actually happening and are updated continuously. Knowing the difference, and knowing which log owns which kind of entry, lets you answer quickly.
