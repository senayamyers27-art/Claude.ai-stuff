Study plans for cybersecurity certifications

Each plan spreads its weeks across the exam's domains by weight and ends with a final review week. Every week has topics, a daily plan, a hands-on lab, study questions and a 10-question quiz. Timed checkpoint tests close each domain, a full practice exam is weighted like the real one, and missed questions come back 1, 3, 7 and 14 days later.

How the questions are written

Questions and plans come from each vendor's official exam objectives, and for Security+ from Senaya's UTD Fullstack Cybersecurity bootcamp notes. Every question shows its source. Answer options are shuffled each time so their position never gives the answer away. Plans marked Weights to confirm haven't been checked against the current official outline yet.
