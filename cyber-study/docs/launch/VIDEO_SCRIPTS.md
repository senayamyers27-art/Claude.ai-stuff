# Short video scripts

Ten scripts of about 30 seconds each (roughly 70 to 80 spoken words) for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and LinkedIn. Each one shows a real part of the site, so you can record it straight from the screen with a voice-over.

Rules for every video:
- Only show features that are live on www.studytocert.com when you post.
- No made-up numbers, pass rates or reviews. If you quote a learner, use a story they shared and agreed to show (Profile, then "Passed your exam?"), and only after you approved it on the Site dashboard.
- Don't say the site is official or endorsed by CompTIA, Cisco, AWS, Microsoft or any other vendor.
- Free features say "free". Paid features say "with Pro" or "with Premium Pro".
- Add captions: most people watch without sound.

Recording tips: phone in portrait (1080 x 1920), screen recorder on, dark mode for contrast, larger text in Settings. Keep each shot to 3 to 5 seconds.
