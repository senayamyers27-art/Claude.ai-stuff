Reservations

Minimum spend and bottle service

VIP tables carry a minimum spend, shown before you request one: $200 for a VIP booth, $250 for a curved booth, $1,000 for the private platform. If your check comes in under the minimum, the difference is charged as a table fee.

Nothing is charged through the app or the website. Everything is settled in person at the venue.

Tell us about an allergy when you book and the kitchen will work around it as far as it safely can.
