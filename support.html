Reservations

How do I change or cancel a reservation?

Use the confirmation code from your booking email/text on our Manage my reservation page to view or cancel it yourself, anytime. Lost your code? Email us and we'll take care of it.

I didn't get a confirmation.

Double-check the email address you entered, including your spam folder. If it's still missing, email us and we'll confirm the booking directly.

How late can I be?

We hold a table 20 minutes past the reservation time. If you're running behind, email or call ahead and we'll do what we can.

Do you take walk‑ins?

Yes, on availability. Join the waitlist in the app and the host will let you know when your table is ready.

VIP tables and bottle service

What does the minimum spend mean?

A VIP table carries a minimum you spend at the table — food, hookah, bottles, anything on the check counts toward it. Booths are $250, the private platform is $1,000, and the standard VIP minimum is $200. Bottles are optional.

Am I charged when I book?

No. Nothing is charged through the app or the website. The minimum is settled in person at the venue.

Can I book a buyout or private event?

Yes — use Events → Private hire in the app, or email support@11elevendallas.com. Monday and Tuesday are available for full buyouts.

The app

How do I install it?

  • iPhone: open the app page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: Chrome offers Install app automatically; otherwise use the browser menu.

It then opens full‑screen with the 11:Eleven icon and still opens with no signal.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Book directly on the site — no password, no separate login. Your confirmation code is all you need to manage a reservation later.

The app is showing an old version.

Close it fully and reopen it with signal. On iPhone you can also remove it from the home screen and add it again — nothing is stored on the device that you would lose.

It will not load.

The app needs a connection the first time you open it. After that it works offline, but live availability will not update. If it still fails, tell us what phone and browser you are using at support@11elevendallas.com.

Privacy and your information

What we collect and how to have it deleted is set out in our Privacy Policy, and the rules around reservations and minimums in our Terms of Use. To see, correct or delete your information, email support@11elevendallas.com.

House rules

  • 21 and over after 9 PM. Valid government‑issued ID required.
  • Elevated dress code — no athletic wear, no sportswear.
  • Hookah service ends 30 minutes before close.
